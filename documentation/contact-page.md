# Contact Page

- Displays information about the collaborators who built the application.
- Each team member is shown as a card with their name, role, and optional details such as university, graduation date, major/minor, and an email link.
- Layout is responsive: cards wrap from one column on small screens to multiple columns on wider displays.
- No interactive form by default; the page simply lists the contributors.
- This page is accessed via the "Contacts" tab in the navigation menu (route `/contacts`).
- Future enhancements may include headshots, social links, or a contact form if needed.
